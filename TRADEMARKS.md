The source code in Host39 repositories is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0) unless a repository says otherwise. That license covers code only. It does **not** grant any rights to the Host39 name, logos, icons or other brand assets.

## What is covered

- The name "Host39"
- Everything in the [`brandkit`](brandkit) folder: logos, the "39" badge, wordmarks, icons, banners, avatars, colors as used together as a brand identity, and the brand guidelines
- Any other logo or mark that identifies a Host39 project

All rights to these are reserved.

## What you may do without asking

- Refer to Host39 and its projects by name, truthfully, for example "runs on Host39 taskies" or "a fork of host39/beats"
- Show the logo unmodified when writing about, reviewing or linking to Host39 projects
- Use the brand assets when contributing to Host39 repositories

## What needs written permission first

- Using the Host39 name or logos in the name, branding or marketing of your own product, service, company, domain or social account
- Offering a modified version, fork or hosted service under the Host39 name or with Host39 logos
- Selling merchandise or anything else that carries the Host39 name or logos
- Anything that suggests Host39 endorses, sponsors or is affiliated with you when it is not

## Forks and modified versions

The AGPL-3.0 lets you fork and modify the code under its terms. If you distribute or host a modified version, remove the Host39 name and logos from it and give it your own name. You may still state that it is based on the original Host39 project.

This is the "declining to grant rights under trademark law" allowed by section 7(e) of the AGPL-3.0.
