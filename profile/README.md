## What is Host39?

I have been homelabbing for ages. Host39 is where I share what I have learned and what I have built along the way.

The goal is simple. Build good, self-hostable alternatives to everyday tools, host or adapt existing open-source projects where that makes more sense, and document how a real homelab is put together. If you are just getting into homelabbing, or you have been at it for years and want fewer cloud subscriptions, you should find something here worth running.
